--- note on the empty upstream count above ---
vLLM writes its access-log line when a request completes. The container was killed while the
generation was still in flight, so the line for that POST was never written. The count is therefore
empty by construction and is NOT evidence of zero upstream requests. The evidence that no replay
happened is the client side: one client call, one error, 4.2 s wall clock, no second completion.
